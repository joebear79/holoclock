HoloClock Support

A holographic clock for Apple Vision Pro, by JoeBear79 Studio.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email JoseRamosMorenoJr@icloud.com and include your visionOS version and what happened.

Common questions

The weather isn't showing.
Allow Location for HoloClock in Settings › Apps › HoloClock. The weather comes from Apple Weather and needs an internet connection.
The alarm didn't go off during a Focus.
Add HoloClock to the allowed apps for that Focus in Settings › Focus. The headset needs to be on for the alarm to ring.
The Desk Buddies don't appear.
Allow surroundings access when asked, and look at a table or the floor for a moment so Apple Vision Pro can find it.
The calendar is empty.
Open the Calendar panel from the HoloClock Calendar widget and tap Allow Calendar.
