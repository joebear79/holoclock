HoloClock Privacy Policy

JoeBear79 Studio · Effective November 2026

HoloClock is made by JoeBear79 Studio. This policy explains what HoloClock does with your information. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and almost everything stays on your Apple Vision Pro.

What leaves your device

Your approximate location, for the weather. When you allow Location, HoloClock sends an approximate location (rounded to about 1 kilometer) to Apple Weather (Apple's WeatherKit service) to get the forecast where you are. HoloClock does not keep your location on any server, does not link it to you, and does not use it for tracking or advertising. Apple's handling of WeatherKit requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

That is the only information HoloClock sends anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, alarm and timer, and the last weather reading (shared only between HoloClock and its own widgets on your device).
  • Calendar (only if you allow it): the Calendar widget and panel read your upcoming events on the device. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Music: Holo Music plays your Apple Music library and files you choose, on the device.
  • Surroundings (only if you allow it): the Desk Buddies use Apple Vision Pro's surface detection to find a table or the floor. This is processed on the device and never stored or sent.
  • Notifications: the alarm and timer are scheduled on the device.

Things HoloClock does not do

  • No sign-in or account
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

Your choices

You can turn Location, Calendar, Media & Apple Music, surroundings access and notifications on or off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security, or in Settings › Apps › HoloClock. Without Location, HoloClock still works; it just can't show your local weather.

Children

HoloClock is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
